# Cookie policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026. ownAplane uses only the cookies it needs to work. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, so we don't show a cookie banner.

## Cookies we set

- **app.session_token** (and related **app.** cookies): keeps you logged in. Strictly necessary. Expires when you log out, or after 7 days without use.
- **locale**: remembers the language you chose. Strictly necessary for the page to appear in your language. Kept for one year.

## Statistics and error reports

If we measure page visits, we do it **without cookies** and without building profiles: we only see aggregated numbers such as how many people opened the search page. Error reports contain technical details about what went wrong, not tracking identifiers.

## Other services

When you sign in with Google, Google may set its own cookies on its sign-in pages; see Google's privacy policy. The map on the search page loads map tiles from OpenFreeMap without cookies.

## Managing cookies

You can delete cookies in your browser at any time; you will then be logged out and the language will follow your browser settings again.
