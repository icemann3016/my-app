# Messages and contact details

Talk to the other side of a booking, or ask an owner about an aircraft before you book, without
sharing your phone number.

## Starting a conversation

- **About an aircraft**: on the aircraft page, **Ask the owner**. One conversation per aircraft
  and person.
- **About a booking**: on the booking, **Message the owner** or **Message the pilot**. Each
  booking has its own conversation.

All your conversations are under **Messages** (the speech bubble in the header shows how many
have unread messages; also in the account menu). An open conversation updates itself every few
seconds. **Ctrl+Enter** (or **⌘+Enter**) sends.

## Emails

When you get a message you also get **one email**, not one per message. The next email comes
only after you've read the conversation.

## Phone and email

Your **phone number** (Account → General → Contact phone, international format like
+359 88 123 4567) and your email are shown to the other side **only once a booking is accepted**,
on the booking page under **Contact the owner/pilot**. Before that, please use messages and don't
share contact details.

## Reporting

Something abusive or suspicious? Use **Report** under the message, on a profile or on an aircraft
page. Only you and our admins see your report; admins see a message only when it's reported.
