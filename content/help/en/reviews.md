# Reviews and ratings

After a completed rental the pilot reviews the aircraft and its owner, and the owner reviews the
pilot. Reviews help owners choose pilots and pilots choose aircraft.

## Writing a review

When the owner confirms the flight log, the booking is **completed** and both of you get a
notification. Open the booking: **Write your review** is at the top.

- **Pilots** rate **aircraft condition** (clean, working, as described), **communication** and
  **value**.
- **Owners** rate **airmanship** (careful handling of the aircraft), **punctuality**,
  **communication** and **condition returned**.
- Give each category 1 to 5 stars. The overall score is their average. A comment is optional.
- You have **14 days** after the owner confirmed the flight log. One review per side per booking,
  and it can't be changed afterwards.

## Double-blind

Neither side sees the other's review until **both** have written one, or until the 14 days are
over. That way nobody can answer a review with a revenge review. Until then your review says
"Only you can see this".

## Where reviews appear

- The **aircraft page** shows its reviews, the average and the average per category.
- **Public profiles** show the rating **as a pilot** and **as an owner**, with the reviews.
- Owners can require a minimum pilot rating in the rental requirements.

## Replies and reports

- The owner can **reply** publicly, **once**, to a review of their aircraft.
- Anyone logged in can **report** a review (abuse, personal data, spam, false information). Our
  team hides reviews that break the rules, but **never just because they're negative**. Hidden
  reviews don't count in the averages.
